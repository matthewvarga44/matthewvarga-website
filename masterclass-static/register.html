Matthew Varga
Founder, The Ordinary Millionaire · 9 properties · Airbnb Superhost

In the 90s Matthew watched his family go through foreclosure and job loss. He spent the next 20 years in banking, as a bank manager, a mortgage broker and a certified financial planner, sitting across the desk from people who earned good money and still couldn't see a way out.

In 2017 he and Nikole had $10,000 in a savings account. Their first fourplex lost $100,000 when oil crashed. They kept going anyway. Five years later they had 9 properties and $1.8M in equity, and at 38, when their first child was born, Matthew left the bank. They've lived off rentals since. On Thursday he shows you the order he'd do it in if he started again.
